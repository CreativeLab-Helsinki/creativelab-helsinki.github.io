The vision

What this place is for, in our own words.

Scene 1 — INT. Helsinki studio

We are building a community of filmmakers. Our mission is simple: to provide the tools, the stage, and the expertise for you to master screenwriting and film production.

In this space, we collaborate. We develop empathy. We create magic.

Fade out.

The curriculum

Three programmes that run alongside each other. You can follow one or move between them as your project grows.

01Writing

Screenwriting

From logline to final draft. Learn structure, dialogue, and character arcs. Ends with live readings at Viirus Theater.

Screenplay readings
02On set

Production

Hands-on filmmaking. Storyboarding, lighting, and directing. We shoot with professional crew and gear.

Short movies
03Lectures

Cinema Talks

Analyze the masters. Deep dives into film history and theory to sharpen your visual vocabulary.
